## The problem {#problem}

Work or contributions are usually recorded only inside the platform where they were done. When the person wants to show them somewhere else, they depend on that platform or have to start from scratch.

## How the protocol solves it {#solution}

The fingerprint of the contribution and who made it are recorded on the chain. The person can present the proof wherever they want. Whoever receives it checks it directly.

## An example {#example}

Ana contributes code, data or results across different projects.

- Each contribution leaves a signed fingerprint.
- Later she can show what she did without asking the platforms for confirmation.
- Whoever evaluates it verifies it in seconds.

The same pattern works for hours, deliverables or any contribution a person wants to carry with them.
