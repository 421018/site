## Risks of use {#risks}

Using the network has risks worth understanding before taking part:

- **Loss of access.** If an account's recovery phrase or private keys are lost, there is no way to recover access.
- **Final transactions.** A confirmed transaction cannot be reversed.
- **Third-party contracts.** Contracts published by third parties can contain errors or be made to deceive. The protocol runs them exactly as written.
- **Errors in the software.** The protocol software is reviewed, but it can contain errors that have not been found yet.
- **Upgrades.** Upgrades approved by governance may require those who run nodes or integrated services to update their software in time.
- **Regulation.** Each person and organization is responsible for complying with the laws that apply to them when using the network and its tools.

## Privacy {#privacy}

This documentation is built so that as little data as possible passes through it:

- **No accounts or forms.** The documentation does not ask for personal data.
- **No cookies or analytics.** It does not use cookies or analytics or advertising tools. It only stores two preferences in your browser, the theme (light or dark) and the selected network, which never leave it.
- **Local search.** The search index is downloaded from the site itself, and what you search for is not sent to any server.
- **Network queries.** The fee calculator and the Console read public network data, such as the latest block or the gas price, straight from your browser from the public services (RPC) of the independent providers in the [directory](/get-started/connect#providers). Those providers receive your IP address, as with any connection, under their own policies. Nothing else is sent.
- **No other third-party resources.** Fonts and other resources are served from the site itself. Apart from those queries, the browser connects to another site only when you follow an external link.
- **Hosting.** Like any website, the hosting provider ([hosting provider]) processes technical data of each visit, such as the IP address, in order to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, under its own privacy policy.

These terms also apply to the Console, which only keeps the selected network in your browser.

Everything recorded on the network, such as addresses, transactions or contracts, is public and permanent, and cannot be erased. That is why personal data should not be recorded on the chain. [What goes on and off the chain](/build/applications#application)
